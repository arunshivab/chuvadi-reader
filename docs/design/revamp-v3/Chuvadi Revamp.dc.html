Proposal · 1a

Four verbs, one document.

Today the same file can live in three places — Reader, Bench, Redact — and every tool is on screen all the time. The revamp turns the app around: there are no destinations, only open documents, and each one has four intents — Read, Mark, Arrange, Protect. Only the tools for the current intent are visible. Click around: tabs, the four verbs, the wordmark (home) and Ctrl K.

No side rail. 8 destinations → 0. Word & Excel just open as tabs; Library lives on Home.
Bench and Redact become modes. Arrange and Protect act on the open document — no hand-offs, no losing your place.
One copy of each control. View controls live only in the floating leaf bar; settings for a selection live only in the right margin.
The page ribbon. Thumbnails and bookmarks merge — chapters show as dividers between pages.
Ctrl K does anything. Every one of the 80+ features can be found by typing it.
Same manuscript identity. Cream paper, binding red, Instrument Serif and the ச wordmark all stay.

Nursing Practice · Policy NP-114

Central Venous Line Care

1. Purpose

To standardise the insertion, maintenance and removal of central venous catheters across all inpatient wards, and to reduce catheter-related bloodstream infection.

2. Insertion bundle

Every insertion follows the five-point bundle and is recorded on the line sticker:

  • Hand hygiene immediately before the procedure
  • Maximal sterile barrier precautions
  • 2% chlorhexidine in 70% alcohol for skin preparation
  • Subclavian site preferred where clinically appropriate
  • Daily review of line necessity

3. Daily care

Inspect the site every shift. Transparent dressings are changed every seven days, or sooner if damp, loose or soiled. Document the score on the visual infusion phlebitis chart.
