Nursing Practice · Policy NP-114

Central Venous Line Care

1. Purpose

To standardise the insertion, maintenance and removal of central venous catheters across all inpatient wards, and to reduce catheter-related bloodstream infection.

2. Insertion bundle

Every insertion follows the five-point bundle and is recorded on the line sticker:

  • Hand hygiene immediately before the procedure
  • Maximal sterile barrier precautions
  • 2% chlorhexidine in 70% alcohol for skin preparation
  • Subclavian site preferred where clinically appropriate
  • Daily review of line necessity

3. Daily care

Inspect the site every shift. Transparent dressings are changed every seven days, or sooner if damp, loose or soiled. Document the score on the visual infusion phlebitis chart.
